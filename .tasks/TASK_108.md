## §A — the spine: what 26 patterns actually say

**Organise by CLAIM, not by pattern.** For each, the evidence, the exceptions,
and the honest scope. My reading of the threads is below — ⚠ **it is a manager's
reading, it is not measured, and the manager's readings have a poor record in

1. **Safety is usually free, and where it is not, the reason is usually not the
   check.** Findings 1, 2, 3, 9, 16; `p24` byte-identical at shipped shape;
   `p46`'s `0.00000`/MAC. ⚠ **The interesting half is the MECHANISM: what the
   optimiser needed in order to delete the check, and what stopped it.**
2. **Where it is not free, name what you are paying for.** `p19`'s `6.25 Ir`/byte
   is `3.00` check **plus `3.25` of foreclosed unrolling**; `p23`'s tax is a
   function of the data's **shape**; `p03`'s is the price of the optimiser
   failing the invariant. ⚠ **"The bounds check costs X" is almost never the
   right sentence, and saying why is a result.**
3. ⚠⚠ **WHERE SAFE RUST DOES NOT HELP — and this is the cluster the project
   under-tells.** `p22` (non-termination: safe Rust hangs too), `p04` and `p09`
   (in bounds, invisible to a memory-safety proof, **Miri-clean**), the
   allocator-recycling result (`.memory/01-ladder.md`: safe Rust's temporal
   guarantee is a guarantee about **the allocator**, so a structure that recycles
   its own storage gets none), and `p34` (**the safe rung is WORSE than C**).
   **Give this its own section. It is the most useful thing here for a reader
   deciding anything.**
4. **What a proof buys and what it does not.** A proof costs **zero executed
   instructions** (finding 1, with `p36`'s vtable scope clause) — **and it
   discharges exactly what it says and no more.** ⚠⚠ **TWO CLEAN INSTANCES OF ONE
   FAMILY, AND A THIRD THAT IS INSTRUCTIVE PRECISELY BECAUSE IT COLLAPSED:**
   `p47`'s proof certifies a **leaking** kernel; a termination proof with a
   `decreases` clause verifies `3/0` while the binary dies of **stack overflow**
   at depth 1e6 (`TASK_102_REPORT`). **Name the family.**
   ⚠⚠ **AND THEN TELL THE `p42` STORY PROPERLY, BECAUSE IT IS THE MOST HONEST
   THING IN THE PROJECT.** `p42` was built, published as *"the first pattern
   whose R5 proof does not cover its own bug class — Verus at the pin CANNOT
   state leak-freedom"*, landed in `.memory/`, **and refuted by its own review

## §B — ⚠⚠ THE CORRECTIONS ARE A RESULT. GIVE THEM A SECTION, NOT A FOOTNOTE.

**This project's most valuable output is the list of things it published and
then had to retract**, because every one is a trap the next person will fall
into:

- **Five patterns published a headline in the FLATTERING direction** — `p10`
  (*"safe beats unsafe"*: 60% an unsearched R4 side), `p27`, `p38`, `p22`
  (**`+2.00` published against a true `+125/+1021` — 510×**), and `p36` in
  mirror image. ⚠ **The discipline that fixed it — search both sides, count the
  levers, name the weaker-searched endpoint — is the transferable part.**
- **`p06`'s `Ir` column was SIGN-WRONG.**
- ⚠⚠ **A within-band holdout proves nothing**: `p23` produced **three mutually
  inconsistent *exact* laws**, and the published one mispredicted the **shipped**
  inputs by `152 Ir`/call despite `0.0000` in-band. **Residue-class trap, three
  times (p38, p46, p23).**
- **A `required` spelling pin can be satisfied by a tautological conjunct the
  compiler deletes**, which moved an in-contract floor `150 Ir`/call and made two
  spans **overlap**.
- **`±7 Ir` per rung is decided by the length of the environment block**, so
  *"re-run the gate and compare"* is not a reproduction test for an `-O3
  isolated` marginal.
- **Seven controls in this project could not have failed** (list at the end of
  `.memory/03-measurement.md`).
- ⚠⚠ **A `required` entry with NO BACKTICKS PINS NOTHING, and every backtick in
  an `idiom` entry IS a pin — including inside explanatory prose.** `p42` shipped
  a `spec.md` claiming three things were pinned when **none of the three was
  enforced, including the idiom the pattern is named for**; separately its first
  gate run failed on backticked words inside a `forbidden` entry's *explanation*.
- ⚠ **A number only a rebuild can produce must not be transcribed into a file the
  rebuild re-hashes.** Four gate runs gave `7, 7, 8, 8` distinct checksums
  because `NOTES.md` is inside the gate record's own `source_sha256`. **Three
  instances across two patterns. Publish the invariant, not the number.**
- ⚠ **A published span's endpoints are what someone thought to write, not what
  the declaration permits.** `p23`'s floor moved 150 `Ir`/call on a tautology;
  `p42`'s R4 endpoint moved 210 / 8707 **and reversed the sign**, turning a
  comparative headline into *"the two admissible classes are not separated"*.
  ⚠ **Two overlapping spans do not become a difference by choosing an endpoint.**

⚠ **Write these as METHOD, addressed to someone building a benchmark — not as
confession.** The tone to avoid is apology; the tone to hit is *"here is what
this measurement will do to you."*
